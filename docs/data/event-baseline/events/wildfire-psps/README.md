# Wildfire and PSPS candidate evidence

Five independent California wildfire candidates are retained here: Kincade (2019), CZU (2020), Glass (2020), Dixie (2021), and Mosquito (2022). Each wildfire bundle cross-references the versioned MTBS burned-area-boundary release alongside its public CAL FIRE incident record; each also retains a bounded EAGLE-I receipt with URL, license, ETag, byte count, and raw/filtered SHA-256.

The bounded EAGLE-I range captures are provenance only: they do not establish absence, partial coverage, or a denominator conclusion until a full annual-source scan is available. None is accepted: these captures do not manufacture county-weather coverage from a fire narrative, and no computed customer-denominator label is asserted.

PSPS is separately labelled only as planned deenergization or co-occurrence. It is not an outage label, wildfire cause, or verified infrastructure-damage finding. The CPUC-hosted PG&E October 2019, August 17–19 2021, and September 20–21 2023 post-event reports are retained in their separate PSPS bundles; no report is used here to infer damage or causation.
