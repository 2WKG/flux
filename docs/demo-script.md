# Flux five-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:30 — Decision

“Flux compares two illustrative 300 MW firm-generation additions under the same fixed stress. The question is not whether either location is real-world deployable; it is where the modeled system has less unmet demand under this synthetic scenario.”

## 0:30–1:30 — Baseline

Select **Baseline**. “The network, the cold-weather stress, and the four-hour duration are fixed. In this fixture the system has 188 MW of modeled shedding. The colored lines are saved synthetic branch-loading values, not live telemetry.”

## 1:30–3:15 — Candidate comparison

Select **Candidate A**. “A reduces modeled shedding by 137 MW, or 548 MWh over this four-hour snapshot.”

Select **Candidate B**. “B reduces modeled shedding by 106 MW, or 424 MWh. Both use the same 300 MW nominal addition and availability assumption, so the difference comes from the illustrative network placement.”

## 3:15–4:15 — Evidence and limits

Open **Sources & limits**. “Everything here is deliberately synthetic until the source case is acquired. This is not a reconstruction of Uri, an outage forecast, a real interconnection assessment, or a licensing decision.”

## 4:15–5:00 — Close

“The result demonstrates the comparison workflow: hold the stress constant, compare alternatives, and preserve the signed result. The next step is replacing this fixture with a supported case and source-backed candidate inputs before drawing any real-world conclusion.”
