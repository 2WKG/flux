## Top 3 remaining risks

1. **Transformer overload path is untested.** 847 branches are `net.impedance` rows with no `loading_percent`; the loop's own `p/sn_mva` computation is now specified but nothing has run it, and the cascade will be silently lines-only if it is skipped (the new third break-it probe in spec 03 §11 is the only guard).
2. **The candidate set is ~12 sites, 4 of them outside ERCOT.** The demo's "ranked Texas coal sites" story is thinner than written, and it depends on the PUDL/EIA-860M pull confirming the owner-announced statuses collected here from secondary sources (Wikipedia roll-up, press releases).
3. **Fragility and derate priors are unsourced**, and both grid-value deltas and the Uri 10–30 GW plausibility band rest on them; the OR-SAGE thresholds are now primary-sourced but the population downgrade for Part 50/52 sites rests on a proposed rule (91 FR 44560) whose comment period closed Aug 31, 2026 and which is not final.
